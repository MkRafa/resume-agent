# Senior Backend Engineer, Payments Platform — Meridian (Remote, India)

Meridian builds payment infrastructure for marketplaces. We're hiring a Senior
Backend Engineer to own the money-movement path.

## What you'll need
- 5+ years of backend engineering experience
- Strong Go — our entire payments stack is Go
- Production experience with Kubernetes
- Experience with event-driven architecture (Kafka, or similar)
- Deep familiarity with relational databases, especially Postgres
- Experience working on payments, ledgers, or financial reconciliation
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or equivalent practical experience

## Bonus points
- Terraform or other infrastructure-as-code
- Experience with gRPC
- Open-source contributions
- Exposure to PCI-DSS compliance work

## About the role
You'll join a team of 8 engineers. You will be expected to mentor mid-level
engineers and drive technical decisions across team boundaries. Strong
communication skills and attention to detail are essential. Must be authorized
to work in India.
